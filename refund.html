Refund Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers books you buy directly from Little Ladybug Press through littleladybugpress.com. Books bought from Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Bookshop.org, or a bookstore follow that seller’s policy, not ours.

Thirty days. If you are not happy with your order, email littleladybugpress@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery. Tell us your order number and what went wrong. We will make it right.

Damaged, defective, or wrong book. Send us a photo of the book and the packaging. We will ship a replacement or refund you in full, including shipping and any sales tax. You do not need to return the damaged copy unless we ask.

Changed your mind. The book must be unread and in resalable condition. Email us first so we can expect the return. You pay return shipping. Once the book arrives in resalable condition, we refund the book price, the original shipping, and any sales tax. Return shipping is not refunded.

Lost in transit. If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or the carrier marks the package lost, contact us. We will replace the order at no cost or refund you in full.

How refunds are paid. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. We issue them within 2 business days of approval or of receiving the return. Your bank may take another 5 to 10 business days to show the credit.

What we cannot refund. Books that were read, written in, water damaged, or otherwise used. Books damaged after delivery. Orders past the 30-day window, unless required by law.

Canceled orders. If we have not shipped yet, email us and we will cancel and refund in full. Once a package is with the carrier, the return steps above apply.

Your legal rights. This policy does not limit any rights you have under Texas or federal consumer law, including implied warranties that goods are as described and fit for their ordinary purpose.

Questions about a return? Write to littleladybugpress@gmail.com or to Little Ladybug Press, LLC, 7607 Berrycone Cv., Austin, TX 78750, United States. We answer within one business day.
