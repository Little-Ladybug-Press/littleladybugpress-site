Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Little Ladybug Press, LLC is a small publisher run by Vandana Rawal and Paige Suffredini. This page explains what we collect when you visit littleladybugpress.com or order a book, why we collect it, and what we never do with it.

What you give us. When you order: your name, email address, shipping address, quantity, and any note you choose to add. When you email us: whatever you put in the message.

Payment information. Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card number, CVC, and billing details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us. We receive only the result: that you paid, the amount, the last four digits, and the card brand. Stripe’s own privacy policy governs its handling of your data.

What is collected automatically. Our host, GitHub Pages, and our security and DNS provider, Cloudflare, keep standard server logs: IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps. These are used to keep the site up and to block abuse. They are not used to build a profile of you.

Cookies and tracking. We do not run advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or social pixels. Stripe may set cookies on its own checkout page to process your payment and prevent fraud. Cloudflare may set a security cookie when it screens traffic. Your browser’s “do not track” setting is honored in the sense that we do not track you across other sites at all.

Why we use your information. To pack and ship your order, email your receipt and tracking, answer your questions, keep the tax and accounting records the law requires, and detect fraudulent orders. We do not sell your data, and we do not send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with. Only the services needed to run the shop: Stripe for payments, our shipping carrier for delivery, Cloudflare and GitHub Pages for hosting and security, our email provider for order messages, and our accountant for tax filing. Each receives only what it needs. We also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it. Order and payment records are kept for seven years to satisfy tax and accounting rules. Server logs are kept about 30 days. Emails are kept while your order is active and for a reasonable period afterward. We delete or anonymize anything we no longer need.

Your choices. Write to littleladybugpress@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. We will respond within 30 days. We may keep records that tax law requires us to keep, and we may need to verify your identity before acting. If you are a California resident, you also have the rights to know, correct, and delete under the CCPA, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising them. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law.

Children. The site is meant for adults buying books. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security. The site uses HTTPS. Payments run on Stripe’s hosted checkout, so card data never touches our pages. Access to order records is limited to the two of us. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the surface small on purpose.

Where your data lives. We sell and ship within the United States only. Our providers process data in the United States.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be noted on this page.

Questions? littleladybugpress@gmail.com, or Little Ladybug Press, LLC, 7607 Berrycone Cv., Austin, TX 78750, United States.
