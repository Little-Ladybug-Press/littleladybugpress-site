The book

Discovery

Lily wakes up thinking about Busy Buzzy. She wants it at breakfast, after school, all day. She even calls it her best friend!

Then Rosie arrives with a different idea: let’s play outside. Out in the yard, Lily starts to realize that she can’t experience the true magic of nature by just looking at a screen — cool grass under bare feet, the smell of a real rose, and so much more.

Written and illustrated by two pediatricians with decades of experience, Lily’s New World was created to open the door to conversations about balance, human connection, and the irreplaceable value of real-world play.

About us

Two pediatricians, one vision

Vandana Rawal, MD

Vandana is a board-certified pediatrician in Austin, Texas, with decades of experience caring for children and families. Lily’s New World! is her picture-book debut.

Paige Suffredini, MD

Paige is a board-certified pediatrician in Austin, Texas, who brings a creative eye to the story as its illustrator, pairing her clinical understanding of child development with warm, engaging artwork.

Dr. Rawal and Dr. Suffredini have served together as pediatricians in Austin, Texas, for over 25 years. They are passionate about empowering families to create a healthy life balance for their children, especially in a culture increasingly tied to electronics.

This book serves as a fun and gentle encouragement for children everywhere to step away from their screens and experience the world around them in countless joyful ways, including through outdoor play and healthy interpersonal interactions.
