About the book

Lily’s New World!

Lily loves her screen. She calls it Busy Buzzy and can’t imagine a day without it. But when her friend Rosie comes over and pulls her outside, Lily discovers something Busy Buzzy simply can’t give her.

This is a warm, whimsical picture book about connecting with friends, stepping away from screens, and stepping into the joys of nature.

Written and illustrated by two pediatricians with decades of experience, Lily’s New World! was created to open the door to conversations about balance, human connection, and the irreplaceable value of real-world play.

Our story

Two pediatricians, one vision

Vandana Rawal, MD

Vandana is a pediatrician in Austin, Texas. Alongside her passion for medicine, she holds a deep belief in the power of mindfulness to help children connect with themselves and experience the world with greater awareness, kindness, and curiosity. Lily’s New World! is her picture-book debut.

Paige Suffredini, MD

Paige practiced pediatrics in Austin, Texas, for more than three decades. Her love of children and understanding of child development now inspire her warm, engaging illustrations. Deeply connected to the message of Lily’s New World!, she hopes her artwork brings the story to life and encourages young readers to explore, imagine, and discover the world around them.

We hope to inspire children and adults alike to share this book, fostering a love of nature and creating meaningful moments of connection and imagination.
